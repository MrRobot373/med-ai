# Hypertension in Adults — Summary

_Demo reference summary. Verify against current ACC/AHA, ESC or national guidance._

## Measurement
Diagnosis should rely on multiple properly measured readings on separate occasions, ideally confirmed with home or ambulatory monitoring to exclude white-coat hypertension.

## Targets
For most adults, including those with diabetes or CKD, a target below 130/80 mmHg is recommended when it can be achieved safely. Older or frail adults may need individualised targets.

## Treatment
Lifestyle: sodium reduction, weight loss, regular physical activity, limiting alcohol, and a diet rich in fruit and vegetables.
First-line drug classes: ACE inhibitors, ARBs, dihydropyridine calcium-channel blockers and thiazide-like diuretics. ACE inhibitors or ARBs are preferred in diabetes with albuminuria and in CKD. Do not combine an ACE inhibitor with an ARB.
Most patients need two or more agents; single-pill combinations improve adherence.

## Resistant hypertension
Blood pressure above target despite three agents including a diuretic at optimal doses. Check adherence and measurement technique, exclude secondary causes (primary aldosteronism, obstructive sleep apnoea, renal artery stenosis) and consider adding spironolactone if potassium and kidney function allow.
