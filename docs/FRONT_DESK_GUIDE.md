# Front desk guide

You can register patients, manage the day's list, upload reports and print. You **cannot** read clinical notes, results or prescriptions — by design.

## Register a patient
**Patients → Register patient.** Name, date of birth, sex and phone are the important ones. Leave **MRN blank** to number automatically.
- As you type, a yellow box says if the person **may already be registered** (same name with the same date of birth or phone). **Open that chart instead** of creating a second one.
- If you are sure it is a different person (a twin, a relative with the same phone), press **Register as a new patient anyway**.

## The day list
**Today's list** shows who is expected. Add a patient (**New → Add to today's list**), set the time and reason, and mark **Arrived**. Several branches? Use the **branch switcher** at the top: each branch has its own list.

## Upload a report
Open the patient → **Upload a report** (PDF, photo, scan). The system reads it and files it; the doctor is told about abnormal values. If a report belongs to someone else it is held back and the doctor is alerted — you do not need to do anything.

## Move in patients from an old system
**Patients → Import.** Choose the Excel/CSV file, check which column is which, read the list of rows that will be skipped and why, then confirm. Nothing is saved until you confirm.

## Duplicate charts
**Patients → Duplicates** (doctors and administrators) lists charts that look like one person. A merge moves everything into the chart you keep and cannot be undone — type the MRN to confirm.

## Never
Share your password; leave your screen unlocked; sign in as someone else. Everything you do is recorded with your name.
