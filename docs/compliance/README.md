# Compliance pack — drafts

> **These are working drafts prepared to save a lawyer's and a regulatory consultant's time. They are not legal advice
> and are not approved.** Each needs review by the person named in its header before it is used with a customer or a
> regulator. Dates and rule numbers change; every statement about a law should be re-checked against the current text.

| Document | Needs review by | Used for |
|---|---|---|
| [DPDP_READINESS.md](DPDP_READINESS.md) | Data-protection lawyer | Privacy law obligations → what the product does → what the clinic must do; patient notice text |
| [DEVICE_REGULATORY_POSITION.md](DEVICE_REGULATORY_POSITION.md) | Regulatory-affairs consultant | Whether and how the software is a medical device; the evidence file |
| [HAZARD_LOG.md](HAZARD_LOG.md) | Clinical Lead + Pharmacist | Patient-safety risks, controls built in, residual risk |
| [INCIDENT_RESPONSE.md](INCIDENT_RESPONSE.md) | Lawyer + security lead | Breach and patient-safety incident procedure |
| [TERMS_DRAFT.md](TERMS_DRAFT.md) | Commercial lawyer | Licence terms, service levels, data-processing terms, limitation of liability |
| [INSURANCE_AND_CERTIFICATION.md](INSURANCE_AND_CERTIFICATION.md) | Broker; owner | Cover to buy; certifications worth the money, in order |

What cannot be written by the engineering side: the lawyer's and consultant's opinions, insurer quotes, the audit and
certification fees and dates, signed customer contracts, and the Clinical Lead's and Pharmacist's signatures
(see `../CLINICAL_GOVERNANCE.md`).
